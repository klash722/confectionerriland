About the Decorator

Kerri Lashua • Head Confectioner & Pastry Artist

With more than 18 years of specialized baking and decorating experience, I bring a passionate blend of culinary craftsmanship, artistic detail, and high-volume production discipline to every creation.

From fine-art hand-painted illustration cookies and intricate pastry tip lettering, to multi-tiered wedding showpieces and sculpted 3D illusion cakes, my focus is always on flawless presentation, structural integrity, and strict food safety standards.

Whether fulfilling large-scale corporate commissions for premier brands like Kate Spade and Marc Jacobs, or hand-crafting celebratory cakes for hundreds of guests, I pride myself on prompt, dependable execution and a warm commitment to service excellence.
